# Medium29 – site content/design review

Reviewed Croatian, English and German versions (51 static HTML pages).

## Changes
- Removed redundant labels, metric placeholders, card numbering, decorative dots and lines, repeated image captions, and superfluous CTA decorations.
- Replaced templated sales writing with service-specific and more natural text.
- Revised homepage, service index and six service pages, FAQ, about, gallery, Nautika 540F, maritime signs, and contact pages across three languages.
- Retained accessibility and useful functionality: language menu, floating navigation, lightbox, form, cookie controls, scroll animations and one-time logo reveal.
- Removed misleading generic photos from the Nautika 540F gallery; Nautika page still uses a clearly described illustrative image.
- Removed repeating photo captions and cut the main gallery from 12 to 6 images.

## Remaining approvals before official launch
- Current service availability and product offering (particularly transport, Yamaha sales/service and booklet).
- Real client-provided company/portfolio photographs, replacing ALL illustrative AI-created images used in this demonstration.
- Real official logo or approval of the concept mark.
- Final privacy/terms review, functional contact form test, sitemap/canonical/redirect migration and live-domain preparation.
